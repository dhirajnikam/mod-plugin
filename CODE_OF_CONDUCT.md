# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, agrees to it.

In short: be welcoming and kind, respect differing views, give and take feedback gracefully, and put the community
first. Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private information, and any other
conduct a reasonable person would find unwelcome are not accepted.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/dhirajnikam). Every report is reviewed and handled in confidence. The maintainer may
warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks it, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
